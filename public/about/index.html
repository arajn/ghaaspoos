About Ghaaspoos#

Ghaaspoos (Hindi: grassroots) is a strategic analysis publication that decodes the hidden mechanics behind global events in geopolitics, technology, and finance.

We don’t just report the news. We dissect the power dynamics, follow the money, and map the chess moves playing out beneath the surface.

Our Approach#

Most media covers the what. We focus on the why and the so what.

Every article applies a framework we call Board Analysis – mapping events to a strategic chessboard. Who is forcing the action? Who is reacting? What is the hidden endgame? We contrast the surface-level narrative with the deeper strategic reality, helping readers see past PR headlines to understand how power actually moves.

Who Writes This#

Ghaaspoos is curated by Raj, a technology professional with deep interests in geopolitics, macroeconomics, and the intersection of technology with global power structures. Articles combine human editorial direction with AI-assisted research and drafting, with every piece reviewed for accuracy and analytical rigor.

What We Cover#

  • Geopolitics – Alliance shifts, trade wars, sanctions, and the chess moves between nations
  • Technology – AI, semiconductors, quantum computing, and how tech reshapes power
  • Finance – Market mechanics, central bank strategies, and how institutional money really moves

Our Philosophy#

The world is a chessboard. Most people watch one move at a time. We try to see the whole board.

“Poor people watch one move. Rich people try to analyze the whole board.”
