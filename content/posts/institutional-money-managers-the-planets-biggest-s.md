**The Private Markets Migration: Escaping Public Scrutiny**

Here's what the headlines won't tell you: Institutional capital is fleeing public markets at an unprecedented rate. Private equity, private credit, and private real estate now represent over 40% of major institutional portfolios – double the allocation from just five years ago.

**This isn't about returns. It's about control**.

In public markets, institutions face quarterly earnings calls, SEC filings, and activist investors. In private markets? They operate in complete darkness. No daily price discovery. No pesky retail investors asking questions. **No transparency requirements**.

The chess move here is brilliant: Move assets off the board where no one can see your position, then manipulate the remaining public markets with the information advantage you've created.
